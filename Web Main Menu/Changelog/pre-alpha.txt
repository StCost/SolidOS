Pre-Alpha

Major Features
- Infinite world generation (40 million km)
- Closing interior on vehicles: drive while physical objects stay placed inside; outside physics do not affect interior items — unique interactive vehicle interior while another player drives in the open world
- NPC agents (patrol, idle, faction ally or enemy, flee, chase, die, ragdoll, dismemberment, inventory, melee attack, gun attack, grenade, heal)
- Networked co-op multiplayer (no player limit)
- Voxel terrain: add, remove, and mix materials
- Persistent world (entities, health, position, opened doors, destroyed objects, dead enemies, terrain operations, and more)
- Boxes to store items physically (throw item inside, close box, take box with you). No UI
- Infinite forever saved painting on entities. Temporary painting on large static world geometry (planned to make infinite too)
- Drag physical objects in world space to relocate them. Weight affects how easy it is to move small or large entities

Other Features
- Day-night cycle
- Player character controller (walk, sprint, crouch, coyote time, step up)
- Player controls NPC agent, so behaves similarly to NPCs
- Player inventory with 12 slots (keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 0, -, =)
- Interactable items: take (E), throw (Q), put down (hold Q), rotate (hold Q + scroll)
- Items holstered on body when small enough
- Holstered glowing items (lamp) illuminate the environment
- Level of detail (LODs)
- First person view and third person view (toggle F5)
- Drivable heavy cargo vehicle with walkable interior
- Weather, randomized or localized (wind, rain, thunder, lightning)
- Energy fuel consumption and refilling. Repair tower station catches lightning to charge battery. Use battery to power vehicle or weapon fabricator
- Water physics and effects
- Moving platforms: lift, elevator, door, beam, or any object
- Seat to control vehicle or sit on chair
- Loot generation in openable containers
- Spawn NPCs at location
- Discord and Steam rich presence; Steam friends join UI
- Cloth collider provider and consumer for cloth–world collision
- Dropped items stack with similar items nearby
- Take small creatures as items
- Friendly NPC companion can follow you
